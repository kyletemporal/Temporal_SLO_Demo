This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and remember that the people
reading your issue or review comment are doing this alongside other work.
Harassment, personal attacks and demeaning language are not acceptable in
issues, pull requests, commit messages or any other project space.
