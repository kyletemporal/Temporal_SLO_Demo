# Worker scale benchmark

- Offered load: **40/s** for **2m** per step (last **60s** measured)
- Activity delay: **50ms** — one activity slot sustains ~20 activities/sec

Laptop numbers from a single-container Temporal running all four roles beside the load generator and the whole observability stack. **Read where the curve bends, not the absolute values.**

| Workers | Offered /s | Throughput wf/s | Activities/s | S2S p95 (s) | S2S p99 (s) | Min slots free | Sync match | Poll success | Efficiency |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 40 | 36.04 | 105.24 | 0.2886 | 0.4630 | 3 | 0.3880 | 0.9949 | 1.00 (base) |
| 2 | 40 | 36.16 | 100.23 | 0.1710 | 0.1955 | 16 | 0.6156 | 0.9969 | 0.50 |

## Reading it

**Throughput did NOT scale** — 36.0/s → 36.2/s across the sweep. Added Workers bought little or nothing, which means the bottleneck is not fleet size. Check, in order: per-Worker concurrency (`MAX_CONCURRENT_ACTIVITIES`), then the Temporal server, then the load generator itself. **An autoscaler would make this worse** — more Workers polling a server that is already the constraint adds poll load without adding capacity (that is scenario 8, poller flood).

**Schedule-to-start fell from 0.289s to 0.171s — but throughput did not move.** The fleet got better at picking work UP without getting more work DONE. Queue wait is the easy thing for an added Worker to improve; it is not evidence of added capacity, and on its own it is not a reason to autoscale. An HPA on schedule-to-start would scale up, watch the signal recover, scale back down, and leave throughput exactly where it started. Look at sync match rate and poll success in the same rows before concluding anything about capacity.

### What each column tells you

| Column | Reads as |
|---|---|
| Throughput below offered | The fleet cannot keep up. The gap is backlog being built. |
| S2S p95 rising | Tasks are waiting for a poller. The first symptom of saturation. |
| Min slots free at 0 | Slot-bound. More Workers will not help; raise concurrency. |
| Min slots free high, S2S high | Worker-bound or server-bound, NOT slot-bound. |
| Sync match falling | The queue stopped draining instantly — tasks now round-trip persistence. |
| Poll success falling | More pollers than work. Over-provisioned (see scenario 8). |
| Efficiency below ~0.8 | Diminishing returns. Near the knee of the curve. |
